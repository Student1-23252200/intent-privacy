Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026 • Intent App

Our Core Principle: 100% On-Device & Private

Intent does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you do inside the application stays entirely on your device.

Zero Data Collection

Intent operates completely offline:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, cookies, or telemetry libraries in the app.
  • No Advertising: Intent does not display ads and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No Remote Servers: Intent does not connect to external servers or make network requests.

Screen Time & Family Controls APIs

Intent uses Apple's official Screen Time APIs (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity) solely to provide the mindful pause feature:

  • App Selection: The apps you select to pause are chosen using Apple’s system-provided picker. Intent never has access to the names, bundle IDs, or icons of the apps you select.
  • Activity Data: Intent does not record or transmit your screen time, browsing history, or app usage to anyone.
  • On-Device Storage: All preferences (such as selected duration allowances) are saved locally on your device in secure App Group storage.
